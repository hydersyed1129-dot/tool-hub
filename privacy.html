Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Information you enter into tools

Tool Hub does not operate an account system or send tool inputs to a Tool Hub application server. Text, images, and PDFs used by the tools are processed in your browser. The QR code and PDF features load third-party JavaScript libraries from jsDelivr; those libraries perform the processing in your browser.

Your browser must request the website files from its hosting provider. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers may receive ordinary connection data, such as your IP address and browser request details, under their own privacy policies.

Storage and analytics

The site does not intentionally save your tool inputs, use advertising cookies, or include analytics code. Your browser, extensions, or hosting provider may handle data independently.

External services

Links to GitHub or other external websites leave Tool Hub. Their content and privacy practices are controlled by those services, not by Tool Hub.

Policy changes and questions

This policy may be updated as the site changes. For questions or corrections, visit the Contact page.
